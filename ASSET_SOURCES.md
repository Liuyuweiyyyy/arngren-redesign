# Asset sources

- Product and hero concept images in `dist/assets`: AI generated for this class prototype. They do not represent verified ARNGREN merchandise.
- Interactive tank: original procedural Three.js geometry in `src/components/TankExperience/tank-scene.js`. No third-party tank model or OP-XY artwork is included. It is a stylized RC concept, not a verified Torro product or real assembly diagram. Environment lighting uses Three.js RoomEnvironment (MIT).
